Auto-lock is now armed when you leave the app, and a failed restore is rolled
back.

Leaving Kura and returning quickly could cancel the record that it had been
backgrounded, so the vault resumed unlocked regardless of the auto-lock setting,
including the strictest immediate option.

A restore that is cancelled, interrupted by locking the vault, or fed a
truncated archive is now unwound instead of leaving part of a backup behind.
