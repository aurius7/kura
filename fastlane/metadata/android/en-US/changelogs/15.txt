Auto-lock no longer depends on returning to the app, and a failed restore is
now rolled back.

Auto-lock is armed when you leave Kura rather than only being checked when you
come back. Previously, pressing Home and reopening the app quickly could cancel
the record that the app had been backgrounded, so the vault resumed unlocked
regardless of the auto-lock setting, including the strictest immediate option.
The background timestamp is now recorded as the last vault screen closes, so the
lock fires on schedule while Kura sits in the background.

A restore that is cancelled, interrupted by locking the vault, or fed a
truncated archive is now unwound, instead of leaving part of a backup in the
vault with no way to tell it apart from a complete restore.
