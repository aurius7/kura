Kura now targets Android 16, and changing a setting no longer locks the vault.

With an immediate auto-lock, toggling any setting rebuilt the screen and the
rebuild was mistaken for the app being backgrounded, so the vault locked and
returned you to the lock screen. Settings now keep both the vault open and your
place on the page.

Media is centred on screen with the tags directly beneath it, video playback
retries a decrypt that lost a race with cache cleanup, and the fullscreen
control is an icon that no longer disappears on narrow screens.
