Kura now targets Android 16, and changing a setting no longer locks the vault.

With an immediate auto-lock, toggling any setting rebuilt the screen and the
rebuild was mistaken for the app being backgrounded, so the vault locked and
returned you to the lock screen. Settings now keep both the vault open and your
place on the page.

Media is centred on screen with the tags directly beneath it, video playback
retries a decrypt that lost a race with cache cleanup, and the fullscreen
control is an icon that no longer disappears on narrow screens.

Tag search now works like the booru tag fields it copies its model from.
Suggestions match anywhere in a tag, so miku finds hatsune_miku, they are
ordered with the closest match first and the most used tag next, and tapping
one fills it in. The * wildcard now works where you type it, so small* and
ta*1 search the vault the same way they do on gelbooru and rule34. The
suggestion row is also sized in dp rather than pixels, which makes the chips
readable and tappable, and it says when nothing matches instead of leaving an
empty gap.
